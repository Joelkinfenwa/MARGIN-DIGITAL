About

Small by design. Direct access.

I started Margin Digital because I have spent years on the other side of the agency relationship. I have built businesses, spent my own money on advertising, hired agencies, and been let down by more than one of them.

What I learned is that good marketing is not a flashy figure on a slide. It shows up in the health of the business: what a customer costs to acquire, what they are worth, and whether they come back. I understand those numbers because I have had my own money on the line against them.

Margin Digital is deliberately small. You deal with me, the prices are on the page before you call, every account is in your name, and if you are not happy at any point you get every dollar back. That is the agency I wanted to hire. It did not exist, so I built it.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does the call cost?

Nothing. Thirty minutes, no obligation, and you keep the plan either way.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If at any point you decide Margin Digital is not for you, say so and every dollar you have paid us is refunded. You keep everything that was built or set up. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google and Meta and is not part of what we charge.

Who actually does the work?

Joel does. The person you speak to on the call is the person building the site, running the account, and answering the phone when something comes up.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No lock-in. Monthly plans are month to month and you can stop any month. The website is a one-off fee.

Is there anything extra to pay?

Advertising spend goes directly to Google and Meta from your own account. Hosting and any third-party subscriptions are separate and itemised up front.

Do you work outside Sydney?

Yes. Margin Digital is Sydney based and works with businesses across Australia. Everything is done online and by phone.
