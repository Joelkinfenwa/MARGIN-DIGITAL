Questions

Straight answers.

How much does it cost?

The website is $999 once, invoiced when it goes live. Google Ads or Meta Ads management is $499 a month, and both together as Complete Growth is $799 a month. All prices are AUD excluding GST. Ad spend is separate and paid by you directly to Google and Meta; hosting and any third-party subscriptions are itemised up front. There are no hourly rates and no surprise invoices.

How does the 90-day guarantee actually work?

Before launch we install a tracking number and measure your calls and form enquiries for 30 days. That is your baseline. If, after 90 days on the growth retainer, your tracked enquiries are not higher than the baseline, we refund every retainer fee paid in that period and keep working at no charge until they are. You keep everything we built. The only conditions are that you answer enquiries within the day, send us your content within seven days, and keep the listing and tracking number live.

Do I need to run ads?

No. The foundation is the site, the Google listing and the reviews process, and for many businesses that is enough. Ads are added from month two, only where the numbers justify them, and are paused the moment they stop producing enquiries.

What do I own if I leave?

Everything. Domain, website, hosting access, Google Business Profile, Google Ads and Meta accounts, and the tracking number are all in your name from day one. We are added as a manager and removed when you leave.

Do you work outside Sydney?

Yes. Most of the work is remote and the audit is delivered as a video, so we work with service businesses anywhere in Australia. Sydney clients can meet in person.

What do you need from me?

About an hour: a call to go through your services and areas, your logo and photos, and a list of past customers we can ask for reviews. After that we need you to answer the phone when it rings.
