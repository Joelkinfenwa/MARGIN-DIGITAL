A note from the founder

I started this because I was let down by agencies. More than once.

I have built businesses, spent my own money on advertising and hired agencies to do what I now do. Some were fine. Several were not. The pattern was always the same: a confident pitch, a long report, and no clear line between what I paid and what I got back.

What I learned is that good marketing is not a ROAS figure on a slide. It shows up in the health of the business: what a customer costs to acquire, what they are worth, and whether they come back. I understand those numbers because I have had my own money on the line against them.

Margin Digital is deliberately small. You deal with me, your prices are on the page before you call, every account is in your name, and if you are not happy at any point you get every dollar back. That is the agency I wanted to hire. It did not exist, so I built it.

Questions people ask on the call

Straight answers.

Which service should I start with?

That is what the call is for. If your website is not converting, we start there, because ads sent to a weak page waste money. If the site is sound, we start with Google Ads. I will tell you honestly which one, even if it is the cheaper option.

What does the call cost?

Nothing. Thirty minutes, no obligation, and you keep the plan either way.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If at any point you decide Margin Digital is not for you, say so and every dollar you have paid us is refunded. You keep everything that was built or set up. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google and Meta and is not part of what we charge.

Who actually does the work?

I do. I am the person you speak to on the call, the person building and running the account, and the person you text when something comes up. There is no account manager in between.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No lock-in. Monthly plans are month to month and you can stop any month. The website is a one-off fee.

Is there anything extra to pay?

Advertising spend goes directly to Google and Meta from your own account. Hosting and any third-party subscriptions are separate and itemised up front, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Do you work outside Sydney?

Yes. Margin Digital is Sydney based and works with businesses across Australia. Everything is done online and by phone.
